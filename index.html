Bingo · 3 tiles across, down, or diagonally. Only drops in your name count.

One gift tag per purple

Due to the economic situation in Gielinor, Santa can only afford one gift tag (tile) per purple. If a purple in your name qualifies for multiple tiles, you must pick the tile when you submit it.

However, the same raid can also count toward Coal, A Puppy!, Office Party, or Gift Cards if you meet their requirements.

For example, if you pull claws and an Olmlet during an Office Party raid, you can claim Office Party and A Puppy!, then put the claws toward one eligible tile. That could be Milk and Cookies, Dear Santa if claws are on your wish list, or The Grinch if claws are on the wish list of another raider in your party.

How to enter

Pay the 2.5m GP buy-in and pick 3 purples for your Santa Wish List. Pets, dust, and kits are excluded.
